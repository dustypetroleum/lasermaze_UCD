Ticketing & Admission

Tickets are available starting at 9:00 AM. To manage the 900+ attendees we expect, please follow these rules:

  • Do not join the line prior to your ticket time.
  • The line closes 30–60 minutes before the event ends.
VIP Tickets & Donations

The Experience

Navigate a 15-meter pathway of lasers and photodetectors in groups of 5–6. Your goal: reach the exit without tripping the alarm.

Warning: The room is dark and filled with fog. Not recommended for those sensitive to these environments.

Finding the Maze

Follow the signs to Kemper Hall 1003. The line forms at the west entrance.

[Map of Kemper Hall 1003 and Queue Area]

Rules & Safety

  • No flash photography.
  • Keep moving; no sudden jumps or stops.
  • Stay 6 inches from boundaries to avoid bumping equipment.
  • Exit immediately after completion to the merch booth inside.
