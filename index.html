Safety & Rules

Environment: The room is dark and filled with fog. Not recommended for those sensitive to these conditions.

  • No flash photography.
  • Keep moving; no sudden movements or jumps.
  • Keep 6 inches from boundaries to protect equipment.
  • Exit immediately to the merch booth inside upon completion.
